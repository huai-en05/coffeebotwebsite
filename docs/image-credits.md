# Image credits

Every illustration, doodle and icon on the site comes from a free library.
None were drawn for this project. Colours were changed to match the site's
browns; nothing else was edited apart from cropping and tidying the SVG files.

| What | Where it's used | Source | Licence |
|------|-----------------|--------|---------|
| People illustrations: `coffee`, `plant`, `unboxing`, `sitting-reading`, `meditating`, `strolling` (`assets/doodles/*.svg`) | Landing hero, login pages, side cards, empty AI panel, footer | [Open Doodles](https://www.opendoodles.com) by Pablo Stanley | CC0 (public domain) |
| Squiggles: sun, star, sparkle, flower, arrow, wavy lines (`assets/doodles/marks/*.svg`) | Underlines, KPI tile corners, hero decorations, footer edge | [dddoodle](https://fffuel.co/dddoodle) by fffuel | [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) |
| UI icons (home, bell, sun, rain, leaf, arrows…) | Nav, buttons, step cards | [Oreo Doodle Icons](https://icon-sets.iconify.design/doodle-icons/) by Nicole Tang, via Iconify | MIT |
| Coffee cup icon | Logo | [Pepicons Pencil](https://pepicons.com) by CyCraft, via Iconify | [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) |

The CC BY 4.0 sets need attribution. It's given in the site footer and in
this file.
